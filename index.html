Current Project

Literature study and theory formulation

CSCC: A Testable Architecture for Functional Consciousness

CSCC investigates whether a recurrent causal architecture integrating content representation, self-modeling, memory, planning, agency attribution, and metacognitive monitoring can produce measurable functional capabilities associated with consciousness.

The project does not claim to prove phenomenal consciousness or subjective experience in an artificial system. It focuses on testable internal mechanisms and reproducible experiments.

Project Repository

Research Approach

My work focuses on converting broad theoretical ideas into explicit computational mechanisms, measurable predictions, controlled comparisons, and reproducible experiments.

The current project is being developed gradually through literature analysis, operational definitions, computational modeling, ablation studies, and causal testing.
