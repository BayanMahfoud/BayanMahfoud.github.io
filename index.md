## Current Project

### CSCC: A Testable Architecture for Functional Consciousness

CSCC is a research project investigating whether a recurrent causal architecture integrating content representation, self-modeling, memory, planning, agency attribution, and metacognitive monitoring can produce measurable functional capabilities associated with consciousness.

The project does not claim to prove phenomenal consciousness or subjective experience in an artificial system.
